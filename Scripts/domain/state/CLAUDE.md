# Player Attributes

Scope: `Scripts/domain/state/player_attributes.gd` and its application path
(`SkaterController.apply_attributes`, `SkaterAppearanceCoordinator.apply`).
Design of record is `docs/attributes-v4-plan.md`.

## The model

A build is **BODY + GEAR** (attributes v4 — design of record in `docs/attributes-v4-plan.md`; the tier system is gone): two permanent continuous **body dials** — **Height** (inches, every inch 5'7"–6'8", tables authored at 5 anchors and interpolated; the range was extended 2026-07 by MOVING the end anchors out rather than adding rows, each end row rebalanced so the outer segment's per-inch slope just continues one more inch — so every previously-playable height kept its values and the two new ones genuinely extend each curve) and **Weight** (lbs, bounded per height by a **BMI band 23.0–29.0** floored at an absolute **162 lb** playable mass (two independent floors: the ratio governs 5'11"+, the absolute governs 5'7"–5'10" where a ratio stops meaning anything — 162 is the lightest player in the NHL), so implausible bodies are unrepresentable by construction; neutral 26.5, and the band is asymmetric about it so `frame_t` is piecewise — min/neutral/max pin to 0/0.5/1 at every height. Recalibrated 2026-07 against listed NHL cards: BMI is the right normalizer — the sample's weight-vs-height allometric exponent is 2.01 — but the old flat 24.0 lean floor was set by short players, whose lower tail is truncated by an *absolute* mass bound rather than a ratio, and it wrongly forbade real tall-lean bodies like 6'4"/195. Both edges are set at the distribution's shape, not its most extreme member — each excludes ~2 tail bodies, and neither is stretched to catch its outlier) — plus four lateral **gear slots** (skate profile, blade curve, stick flex, stick length; 0/1/2, 1 = balanced). **There is no power economy**: every axis is lateral, so there is no legal shape, no `is_legal_build` — validation is pure coercion (`coerce_height` / `coerce_weight` / gear clamps; a forged build lands on the nearest legal body). Neutral reference is **6'1" / 201 lbs / all-balanced** (the real NHL-average frame), where every gameplay multiplier is 1.0 (= the shipped `@export` defaults). **Gear slots — three of four live**: **STICK LENGTH** (short/standard/long — a ±4% lean on the height's cut, feeding reach, poke/claim range, the blade-cap lever, the wrister full-stroke rescale, and the stick mesh); **BLADE CURVE** (M88/M92/M28, modeled on the P88/P92/P28 patterns — the blade's **ANGLE LADDER** is the elevation lever (the manual contact-point model, design of record `docs/elevation-rework-plan.md` v3): each loft level 1–3 is a SET launch angle from the pattern's ladder (`_CURVE_LOFT_*_DEG`: LOW 7/8/8.5°, MID 14/15/16°, HIGH 22/26/30° for M88/M92/M28), no solve, no position term, arrival emergent from angle × charge × range with missing high a real outcome — the M88's flattest ladder can never sail (max-slap LOW apexes at crossbar-ping height) but can't roof the crease, the M28 roofs the doorstep and is worse at range on every rung, the M92's 8° LOW is the textbook point snipe; the levels double as deflect modes (`PuckCollisionRules.deflect_loft_speed`); quick passes stay on the fixed-speed table, curve-invariant; curve also leans the backhand coefficient toward-but-never-past parity (1.08/1.00/0.90), the SLAPPER-only power (±3% — pattern-squareness through the heel-contact sweep; wristers stay flex-only), the RECEPTION deflect ceiling + squared bonus (±7% via `Skater.reception_ceiling_mult`, read at the PuckController contact scan and the lag-comp pickup resolver — never `pickup_max_speed`, so soft passes settle on every blade and client provisional pickup stays build-independent), and the wrister runway (M28 0.90, dormant until the travel gate unfreezes). The rendered blade matches the pick — `Skater.apply_blade_pattern` swaps depth / bend position / face twist / toe radius per gear); **STICK FLEX** (whippy/medium/stiff — leans the shot ceiling WITH the slapper wind-up and wrister runway, a true lateral seesaw, deliberately unlike the height coupling where a harder shot also threatens sooner). The stacked quick-release loadout (whippy+open) bottoms at 81% of the neutral runway — the runway floor that keeps a max-power release readable to the goalie (NOTE: the "runway" IS the wrister travel gate, currently DORMANT — see the Wrister bullet; `apply_attributes` still rescales it, but nothing reads it while the blade is frozen); **SKATE PROFILE** (agility/balanced/power — the blade grind: power = +top speed/glide, −agility incl. `lateral_grip`; agility = +first step/cornering, −top speed; this is what re-widens the sprint band the body plane compressed, ~20.5–24 mph across profiles; stacked body×gear agility corners pinned by test at ~0.84–1.15). **All four slots are live.** Bots price their own gear: `AISkaterCaps.loft_tans` feeds the HIGH-hole rung-picker (a bot prices and fires the rungs its own blade actually has), `AISkaterCaps.lateral_grip` feeds the planning models everywhere a direction change is priced (the ETA cross-momentum shed, each defender's `reach_clearance` shed, the deke's lateral bite/unwind budgets — calibration-tested against a gripped real-physics sim), and blade caps/reach/backhand ride the caps as before; the one residual drift (defensive reads of opponents assume an open face — conservative) is noted in the plan doc. The picker is the player screen (`player_settings_popup.gd`, built in code, single-column, main-menu only — the lobby has no build editor): ONE build per player (no presets — "it's just who you are"), with height/weight sliders inline (moving height preserves the frame fraction, so a lean build stays lean as it grows) and two workbench sub-editors — the stick gear rows in `stick_editor_popup.gd`, the blade profile + skate/glove colors in `gear_editor_popup.gd`. The screen also holds the preferred POSITION (C/LW/RW/LD/RD — `PlayerRules.POSITION_NAMES` index, carried by `request_join`), which only steers lobby seating via `PlayerRules.preferred_slot` (3v3 merges LW/LD onto the left slot and RW/RD onto the right); it never touches gameplay. Bots have curated cards in `data/bot_identities.json` (`{height, weight, gear…}` — hockey-card readable). Online matches lock the build at join time (replicated as **6 ints** — height, weight, profile, curve, flex, length — through `request_join` / `spawn_remote_skater`, `PROTOCOL_VERSION` v37); free-play picks re-apply immediately without a respawn.

The routing (the constitution: a lever may scale **outputs** or **geometry**, never **fidelity** — see the plan doc §1):
- **Height** → speed↔agility baseline fork (`max_speed` hump peaks at 6'1"; agility small-favored), **shot-power baseline** (big-favored, ~89.5 mph neutral slapper → ~98.7 mph at 6'8" body-only; gear re-widens the top end later), reach / mesh scale / arm-ROM / stick length (mesh-native 5'10" is the reach-1.0 anchor). *(+ visual height on Y; the head/helmet unit scales only by its own mild table and never stretches with height — near-constant adult heads are what sell a tall build as big rather than zoomed)*
- **Weight** → **mass** (`mass_mult = lbs/201`, LINEAR and wide 0.79–1.28 — with no Checking stat, mass IS the physical system: delivery and brace are body-emergent through the collision resolver's reduced-mass math, `check_delivery_mult`/`brace_mult` are flat 1.0), **accel↔momentum fork** (lean = first-step burst; momentum is mass-emergent), an **agility bite** (F = mv²/r, delivered mechanically by `lateral_grip` — agility scales the perpendicular-to-motion thrust authority in `SkaterMovementRules`, so the turn *radius* itself widens/tightens per build; heavy turns wide and stops long, **glide is exempt** so the tank still coasts, and top speed stays weight-free), **lateral girth** (`girth_mult` — the ONE grounded width: body density is ~constant so girth = sqrt(mass/height), normalized at 6'1"/201; every visual body part — torso, shoulders, limbs — AND the hitbox radius wear it, so silhouette = hitbox by construction, tall-lean reads elongated, short-heavy reads hydrant, and there are no authored width curves left to drift), and **stamina metabolism** (lean = shallow pool/fast regen, heavy = deep pool/slow refill — moved off height). *(silhouette = body: height scales the skeleton, weight-for-height scales the girth)*
- **Hands** → **nothing, by constitution** ("your hands are you"): `hands_blade_mult()`/`hands_backhand_mult()` are 1.0 for every build. Hands differentiation is delivered as lever *geometry*, never a multiplier: the blade caps derive from the build's actual sweep lever (stick + arm ROM, normalized to the 6'1"/standard neutral) — **tip speed ∝ lever** (same angular gesture, faster blade in m/s; traverse time across your own reach stays ~flat, pinned ≤8% by `test_blade_lever_calibration`) and the **inertia cap ∝ 1/lever^k** (`blade_inertia_exponent`, default 1.2) — the long lever sweeps, the short lever cuts back. The second-order blade servo ships **ON** (`max_blade_accel = 250`, `blade_inertia_exponent = 1.6` — playtest-calibrated across the min/neutral/max builds; 0 disables it, reverting to the pre-v4 first-order servo bit-exact).

**Sprint & carry:** the sprint **ceiling** is still speed-normalized over the full v3 span (`sprint_ceiling_mult`), so body-only builds sit in a tight ~22 mph middle band until the skate-profile gear slot re-widens it; the carry penalty is **Speed-eased only** (the v3 Hands term is folded into `CARRY_BASE` so neutral is unchanged at ~0.958); the real cost of carrying at speed remains the **1.6× sprint stamina drain** (`sprint_carry_penalty_bypass` unchanged).

All tuning lives as private tables on `PlayerAttributes`, read via named accessors (`attrs.speed_mult()`, `accel_mult()`, `mass_mult()`, `sprint_ceiling_mult()`, …) — **never index a `_*` table outside that file**. Height tables are 5-vecs at the anchor heights; frame tables are 5-vecs lean→heavy indexed by `frame_t()` (position in the integer pounds band, so band edges and the 73"/201 neutral are exact). Derived/coupled levers are computed from their partner so they can't drift (edge glide = `2 − agility`, slapper wind-up = `2 − shot power`). To add a new "X scales Y" rule, see the doc-block at the top of `player_attributes.gd`.

Application path: `SkaterController.apply_attributes(attrs)` reads the accessors and writes scaled values to controller `@export`s, `Skater.weight`, etc.; `SkaterAppearanceCoordinator.apply(attrs)` does the visual mesh scaling. Both are idempotent — they capture baseline values on first call and recompute from baselines every subsequent call, so repeated applies (free-play picker changes) never compound. **Prefs migration** (`player_prefs.gd`, gated by `attr_scale_version`, now v5): a v4 tier save maps deterministically via `PlayerAttributes.migrate_tiers` (Checking→frame weight, strong Skating→profile lean, strong Skill→open curve or short stick by height, strong Checking→long stick); older six-attribute saves chain through `migrate_legacy` → `migrate_tiers`. Bot rosters and stored presets migrate through the same `from_dict` seam.
