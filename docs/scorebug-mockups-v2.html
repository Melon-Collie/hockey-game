Scorebug v2 — Precision-Sport Direction

Revisiting the scorebug/scoreboard with the clarified gameplay direction: mouse-driven skill expression, not exaggerated arcade, not realism simulation. The HUD has to support fast at-a-glance reading and stay out of the way of precise input. The chyron-as-personality architecture still holds, but the chyron itself may want to feel more "modern indie precision sport" than "1991 broadcast nostalgia." All three variants here drop the cream tint (which encoded vintage) for crisp white, and stay on Bebas Neue. The ice background matches the new in-game cool blue-gray tint so cross-temperature pairing is honest.

Current B+ Vintage broadcast chyron (what we shipped)
Identity: 1991 SportsChannel broadcast. Cream whites, gold accent, hard offset shadow. Strongest retro personality. Pairs cohesively with itself but creates a temperature war with the new cool ice underneath.

My read: Variant D is the safest swing — keeps everything that works about the current scorebug and just modernizes the palette. Variant E commits hardest to "modern sport broadcast" and would feel most cohesive with the gameplay character; the team-color blocks are also great for fast reading. Variant F is the bold move — it pulls the chrome out of the way entirely, but it gives up the chyron-as-identity-layer architecture we just agreed on. If you want the HUD to be a personality element, F is too minimal; if you want the HUD to maximally serve precision gameplay, F is exactly right.

My gut: Variant E. It modernizes the tone, reads instantly as a sport HUD, the team-color blocks are functional, and it shares the "confident modern" vibe of Tape to Tape (which is the only direct comparison in the indie hockey space).
