Scorebug & Scoreboard — Retro Direction Variants

Three points along the "lean retro broadcast" spectrum. Each variant shows the in-game scorebug (positioned in the corner of a faux-ice scene) and the Tab-popup scoreboard. The current design is at the top for reference. All variants use only effects that translate cleanly to Godot's StyleBoxFlat (solid fills, borders, drop shadows, corner radius). The Variant C bevels use linear gradients on title bars — those would need StyleBoxTexture or be approximated with multiple stacked solid fills.
